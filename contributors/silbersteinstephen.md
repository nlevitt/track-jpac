# Contributor Report: Stephen Silberstein

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Stephen "Steve" Silberstein is a California-based political donor and philanthropist. His philanthropic endeavors frequently support left-of-center causes and organizations, with a focus on issues such as income inequality and electoral reform.

Regarding documented relationships to the Israel lobby and pro-Israel advocacy, Stephen Silberstein has been identified as a supporter of J Street. J Street is an organization that defines itself as the "political home for pro-Israel, pro-peace Americans," advocating for a two-state solution to the Israeli-Palestinian conflict.

Evidence of Silberstein's support includes donations from the Stephen M. Silberstein Foundation to J Street. Furthermore, Stephen Silberstein is individually listed as a donor or member within J Street's financial statements and donor lists, including their "Tikva Society," which recognizes leaders committed to J Street's future. One source also broadly categorizes Silberstein as a "supporter of liberal Zionist causes," indicating an alignment with a faction of pro-Israel thought that generally favors a two-state solution and may be amenable to "real pressure on Israel" in peace efforts.
