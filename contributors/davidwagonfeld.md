# Contributor Report: David Wagonfeld

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

David Wagonfeld is a California-based investor and community leader with documented involvement in organizations that support Israel. His engagement includes direct affiliation with pro-Israel advocacy groups and leadership roles in institutions that foster economic and communal ties with Israel.

Wagonfeld's community involvement includes serving with the American Israel Public Affairs Committee (AIPAC). He has stated that a teen trip to Israel following his confirmation sparked a "lifelong connection to Judaism and Israel".

Furthermore, Wagonfeld serves on the Executive Committee and chairs the Finance Committee for the Friends of Ben Gurion University. This organization supports Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, located in Israel.

Wagonfeld is also a co-founder and General Partner at J-Ventures Fund, which has been identified as investing in the "Israeli startup ecosystem".

His past and present affiliations with Jewish Federations also demonstrate a connection to organizations that support Israel. He previously served on the Board of the San Francisco Federation for ten years and is currently a member of the Capital Planning Committee of the Jewish Community Federation & Endowment Fund. The Jewish Federation of the Bay Area has explicitly stated its support for Israel, including mobilizing funds for an "Israel Emergency Fund" and making investments to strengthen Jewish communities in the Bay Area and Israel.
