# Contributor Report: Alan Casden

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Alan Casden, an American real estate developer and philanthropist, has demonstrated support for Jewish organizations and causes, including those with a focus on Israel. Casden is recognized as a prominent supporter of Jewish organizations, contributing millions of dollars to groups such as the Simon Wiesenthal Center, where he holds a position as co-chair of the board of trustees. His philanthropy also includes the establishment of the USC Casden Institute for the Study of the Jewish Role in American Life at the University of Southern California, part of his donations exceeding $10 million to the institution. He has served on the board of the Jewish Federation of Greater Los Angeles, which supports efforts in community welfare, education, and advocacy for Jewish interests.

Casden is noted as a strong supporter of Israel. He was recognized as a "friend of Jerusalem" for his involvement with the International committee of the Jerusalem 3000 celebration. Additionally, in 2010, Casden had plans for a luxury apartment building in Tel Aviv, Israel, though the project was reported to be on hold due to economic conditions at the time.
