# Contributor Report: Foreman, Danielle

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Danielle Foreman serves as the Chief Strategy and Impact Officer at the Jewish Federation Bay Area, an organization that states its mission includes deepening its "footprint in the Bay Area, Israel, and globally". In this executive role, Foreman is involved in shaping the Federation's impact efforts, which explicitly extend to Israel. The Jewish Federation Bay Area is also noted as the "single largest funder of Jewish causes in California".

Foreman's documented professional affiliations include her service on the advisory board of IsraAID. IsraAID is an Israeli-based international humanitarian aid organization. Prior to her current role, Foreman was the Chief Program Officer at the Koret Foundation, where her responsibilities included guiding grantmaking within the Jewish community, with a focus on initiatives related to "Jewish Peoplehood" and "Combat Anti-Semitism".

While these roles demonstrate a documented relationship to organizations engaged in pro-Israel advocacy through community engagement, philanthropic endeavors, and humanitarian aid that strengthen Israel's international standing, a direct and specific record of Danielle Foreman as an individual "political donor" to organizations identified as part of the "Israel lobby" (e.g., AIPAC or J Street) through personal campaign contributions was not found in the available information. Searches for "Foreman" in the context of political donations to pro-Israel PACs yielded results for individuals named "Sara Foreman" and "Keyvone T. Foreman," not Danielle Foreman. Other individuals named Danielle Foreman were identified in unrelated fields such as therapy, sports, and film.
