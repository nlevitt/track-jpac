# Contributor Report: DOUGLAS, MICHAEL

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

**Michael Kirk Douglas** is a prominent American actor, film producer, and political donor based in **Montecito, California**. Known for his extensive career in Hollywood, Douglas has a long-standing and documented relationship with the Israel lobby and pro-Israel advocacy, characterized by high-level diplomatic engagements, financial contributions to Jewish and Israeli institutions, and public campaigning against anti-Israel sentiment on U.S. college campuses.

### 1. High-Level Diplomatic and Institutional Ties
Douglas’s relationship with the Israeli state is formal and established through major awards and personal relationships with Israeli leadership.

*   **Genesis Prize Laureate (2015):** In 2015, Douglas was awarded the **Genesis Prize** (often called the "Jewish Nobel") by Israeli Prime Minister **Benjamin Netanyahu** and then-Jewish Agency Chairman **Natan Sharansky**. The $1 million prize was granted in recognition of his "commitment to the Jewish people and the State of Israel." Douglas opted to donate the prize money to initiatives promoting "inclusive" Judaism and outreach to intermarried families to maintain their connection to the Jewish community and Israel.

*   **Solidarity Missions:** In June 2024, during the ongoing conflict in Gaza, Douglas conducted a "solidarity visit" to Israel. He met with Israeli President **Isaac Herzog**, who presented him with a dog-tag necklace and a yellow ribbon pin, symbols of solidarity with Israeli hostages. During this trip, Douglas visited **Kibbutz Be’eri**, a site of the October 7 attacks, to express his support for the Israeli military and civilian response.

### 2. Pro-Israel Advocacy and Campus Campaigning
Douglas has actively worked to counter the **Boycott, Divestment, Sanctions (BDS)** movement and anti-Israel rhetoric in academic and entertainment circles.

*   **Campus Tours with Natan Sharansky:** In 2016, Douglas partnered with Natan Sharansky to tour several elite U.S. universities, including **Stanford**, **Brown**, and **UC Santa Barbara**. The tour, titled "Jewish Journeys," was designed to strengthen Jewish identity among students and defend the State of Israel against delegitimization and the BDS movement.
    *   [Source: The Stanford Daily](https://stanforddaily.com/2016/02/04/actor-michael-douglas-and-activist-natan-sharansky-talk-jewish-identity/)

*   **Opposition to Campus Protests:** Following the 2024 campus encampments and protests against the war in Gaza, Douglas publicly criticized student activists, describing them as "brainwashed" and lacking "education" and "knowledge" regarding the historical context of the conflict.

*   **Creative Community for Peace (CCFP):** Douglas is a high-profile signatory for the **Creative Community for Peace**, an organization that specifically counters cultural boycotts of Israel. In 2023, he signed their open letter unequivocally supporting Israel's military campaign and condemning Hamas.

### 3. Media and Narrative Influence
Douglas has utilized his professional platform to promote narratives favorable to the Israeli state and Jewish history.

*   **Documentary Narration:** Douglas narrated the documentary **"In Search of Peace"** (2001), produced by Moriah Films (the film division of the **Simon Wiesenthal Center**). The film documents the history of Israel from 1948 to 1967, focusing on its military struggles and survival. He also narrated **"One Day in September"**, which covers the 1972 Munich Olympics massacre of Israeli athletes.
