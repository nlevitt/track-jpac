# Contributor Report: Efrem Harkham

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Efrem Harkham is a prominent Los Angeles-based hotelier, real estate investor, and philanthropist. Born in Israel, Harkham is the founder and chairman of Luxe Hotels and Luxe Worldwide Hotels. His documented pro-Israel advocacy ranges from financial contributions to specific political candidates to formal leadership roles in Zionist organizations and the facilitation of public relations efforts for the state of Israel.

### 1. Leadership and Organizational Affiliations
Harkham has held formal positions in major pro-Israel advocacy organizations, most notably the Zionist Organization of America (ZOA).
*   **Zionist Organization of America (ZOA):** Harkham was designated an honorary board member of the ZOA’s Los Angeles Regional Board in recognition of his "generous contributions" to the organization. He has hosted ZOA meetings and dessert receptions at his Luxe Sunset Boulevard Hotel.
    *   Source: ZOA Report, 2006 (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)
*   **Jewish Television Network:** Harkham serves on the Board of Directors for this network, which produces content related to Jewish life and Israel.
    *   Source: Jewish Journal (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)

### 2. Pro-Israel Advocacy and PR Efforts
Harkham has explicitly stated that he views maintaining a connection to Israel as a personal "commandment" and actively engages in public relations for the country.
*   **Public Relations Trips:** Harkham organizes and hosts annual trips to Israel for non-Jewish professionals (including Catholic, Hindu, and Hispanic participants) to "expose Israel in the best light possible."
    *   Source: Jewish Journal, 2019 (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)
*   **Collaboration with Israeli Leadership:** In his memoir, Harkham recounts a 1982 meeting with then-Israeli Prime Minister Menachem Begin, where he offered to fund a PR campaign for Israel. Begin reportedly advised him to instead direct his support to the **American Israel Public Affairs Committee (AIPAC)** to strengthen the connection between the Diaspora and Israel.
    *   Source: Jewish Journal, 2019 (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)

### 3. Documented Political Contributions
Harkham’s political giving often aligns with candidates who prioritize pro-Israel foreign policy.
*   **Elan Carr for Congress:** In 2014, Harkham donated the maximum individual amount of $2,600 to the congressional campaign of Elan Carr. Carr later served as the U.S. Special Envoy to Monitor and Combat Anti-Semitism and is a well-known advocate for Israel.
    *   [Source: FEC.gov Itemized Receipts](https://www.fec.gov/data/receipts/individual-contributions/?contributor_name=Efrem+Harkham)
*   **General "Israel Lobby" Giving:** Independent research into the Harkham family’s political spending notes that a significant portion of their donations is directed toward political action committees (PACs) focused on "Jewish/Israeli issues."
    *   Source: Cinefamily Accountability / Public Record Analysis (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)

### 4. Community and Philanthropic Support
Beyond direct political lobbying, Harkham provides infrastructure and funding for pro-Israel and Jewish educational causes.
*   **Harkham Hillel Hebrew Academy:** Together with his brother Uri, Efrem donated over $1 million to expand this Orthodox Jewish Day School, which emphasizes Zionist values.
    *   Source: Jewish Journal (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)
*   **Event Hosting:** Harkham frequently hosts pro-Israel events at his hotels, such as the 2015 panel "Israel: What Happens Now?" featuring high-profile speakers on U.S.-Israel relations.
    *   Source: American Friends of Shaare Zedek Medical Center (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)
