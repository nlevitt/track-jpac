# Contributor Report: Marc Berman for Assembly 2018

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Research indicates that the political campaign "Marc Berman for Assembly" has documented relationships with pro-Israel advocacy and the Israel lobby through direct contributions, legislative affiliations, sponsored legislation, and participation in delegations to Israel.

### Financial Contributions

Marc Berman's political campaigns have received documented contributions from organizations and individuals identified with the Israel lobby. According to track-jpac, an organization that tracks Israel lobby contributions, Marc Berman has received a total of $77,550 in direct contributions from sources identified as part of the Israel lobby. Specific contributors to Marc Berman's campaigns, with years spanning or encompassing the 2018 election cycle, include:
*   California Jewish PAC, which contributed $29,050 between 2019-2025. This PAC is described as a primary vehicle for pro-Israel and Zionist advocacy and political influence within California state government.
*   California Jewish Legislative Caucus Leadership PAC, which contributed $13,000 between 2015-2017. This PAC is identified as the political financing arm of a legislative caucus dedicated to promoting pro-Israel legislation, strengthening California-Israel ties, and countering anti-Zionism.
*   Doris Fisher, who contributed $9,900 between 2017-2021. Fisher is noted as a prominent donor supporting pro-Israel advocacy and Israeli institutions through partnerships with the Koret Foundation, contributions to Birthright Israel, and AIPAC.
*   Robert J. Fisher, who contributed $9,600 between 2015-2017. Fisher is described as a prominent donor to Zionist and pro-Israel organizations and institutions, including Hebrew University of Jerusalem, Birthright Israel, and the Koret Foundation.
*   William S. Fisher, who contributed $9,600 between 2015-2017. Fisher is identified as a major donor to Birthright Israel and the Jerusalem Foundation, closely associated with the Koret Foundation (a large funder of pro-Israel causes), and a donor to pro-Israel political candidates.

### Legislative Affiliations and Actions

Marc Berman is a member of the California Legislative Jewish Caucus (LJC). This caucus aims to promote pro-Israel legislation, strengthen California-Israel ties, and counter anti-Zionism. The LJC has issued statements characterizing support for a free Palestine as antisemitic and has been noted for showing public support for Israeli victims of the October 7th attack without similar statements for Palestinian victims. Marc Berman publicly endorsed a statement from the California Legislative Jewish Caucus which denounced protests using chants perceived as calling for the destruction of Israel and justifying Hamas's actions.

Berman has also been a co-author and sponsor of legislation that aligns with pro-Israel advocacy. He is a co-author of Senate Bill 1387 (SB 1387), which proposes adding "Jewish" as an ethnic identity on California state government forms. This bill is sponsored by "Jewish California," formerly known as the Jewish Public Affairs Committee of California (JPAC), a coalition of over 40 Jewish organizations. Additionally, Berman is listed as a sponsor of Assembly Bill 2664 (AB 2664), a bill that seeks to restrict protests at places of worship by creating a 100-foot buffer zone around them. One source describes AB 2664 as part of an "Israel lobby's agenda of indoctrination, censorship and genocide-denial," aiming to curb protests related to sales of occupied Palestinian land or events featuring Israeli military figures.

### Delegations to Israel

In July 2018, Marc Berman participated in a bipartisan delegation of California state legislators that visited Israel. This trip was sponsored entirely by Project Interchange, an educational institute of the American Jewish Committee (AJC). The stated purpose of the program was to strengthen California-Israel relations and explore opportunities for bilateral collaboration.
