# Contributor Report: Lebovics, Irving

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Irving Lebovics is a political donor and community leader in California, primarily known for his role as the Chairman of Agudath Israel of California, a Jewish advocacy organization. Documented evidence indicates his engagement in activities and endorsements that align with pro-Israel advocacy.

In August 2021, Dr. Irving Lebovics participated in and addressed a solidarity dinner that was hosted by Dr. Hillel Newman, the Israeli Consul General of the Pacific Northwest. Lebovics expressed his honor in being part of the event and stated his support for the Consul General's work. This interaction demonstrates a direct documented relationship with an official representative of the State of Israel.

Furthermore, in October 2024, when providing his personal recommendations for upcoming elections, Lebovics explicitly considered candidates' positions on Israel. In endorsing Sydney Kamlager for US House of Representatives District 37, he noted that Kamlager "has been a strong supporter of Israel and the Jewish Community on issues of import and has stood up to extreme pressure from Anti-Israel forces". This highlights his consideration of pro-Israel stances as a significant factor in his political recommendations to the Jewish community.

Lebovics's activities as Chairman of Agudath Israel of California frequently involve advocating for the interests of the Orthodox Jewish community, including concerns related to security and antisemitism. While the primary focus of Agudath Israel of America (the national organization) is often on domestic Jewish communal issues, the California chapter, under Lebovics's leadership, has engaged with Israeli diplomatic representatives, suggesting a broader scope that includes support for Israel within its advocacy efforts. His involvement is also noted by JNS.org, a news agency focused on Israel and the Jewish world.
