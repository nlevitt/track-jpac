# Contributor Report: CABALDON, CHRISTOPHER

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Christopher Cabaldon, a member of the California State Senate representing District 3, has received documented political contributions from individuals identified as prominent donors to Zionist and pro-Israel organizations. According to an analysis by track-jpac, Cabaldon's total Israel lobby contributions amount to $39,500. These contributions are categorized as "Israel lobby direct contributions".

Specific individuals identified as contributors to Christopher Cabaldon, and their documented relationships to pro-Israel advocacy, include:
*   **Robert J. Fisher:** Contributed $9,200 between 2007 and 2025. He is described as a prominent donor to Zionist and pro-Israel organizations and institutions, including Hebrew University of Jerusalem, Birthright Israel, and the Koret Foundation.
*   **Doris Fisher:** Contributed $7,200 in 2007. Through family entities and philanthropic funds, she supports pro-Israel advocacy and Israeli institutions via partnerships with the Koret Foundation, contributions to Birthright Israel and AIPAC, and the funding of Israeli political figures.
*   **Donald Fisher:** Contributed $7,200 in 2007. He is described as a prominent philanthropist who provided significant financial support to Jewish communal organizations and Zionist advocacy, including major donations to Birthright Israel and institutional partnerships with the Koret Foundation.
*   **John Fisher:** Contributed $7,200 in 2007. He is a major donor to AIPAC and the Birthright Israel Foundation, and leverages family foundations to support Zionist identity programming and anti-BDS initiatives.
*   **William Fisher:** Contributed $7,200 in 2007. He is a major donor to Birthright Israel and the Jerusalem Foundation, closely associated with the Koret Foundation (a large funder of pro-Israel causes), and a donor to pro-Israel political candidates.
*   **Richard Leib:** Contributed $1,500 between 2001 and 2023. He is noted as a prominent advocate for Zionist causes who actively used his position as a UC Regent to oppose anti-Zionist activities and maintain ties with pro-Israel organizations.

These contributions constitute the documented relationship between Christopher Cabaldon and individuals associated with the Israel lobby and pro-Israel advocacy. No Israel lobby federal contributions for Cabaldon have been identified.
