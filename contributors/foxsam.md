# Contributor Report: FOX, Saul

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Research indicates that a prominent political donor named Saul Fox, residing in California, has a documented relationship with pro-Israel advocacy through his financial contributions to the "Children of Israel" organization.

In August 2016, Republican presidential nominee Donald Trump held a fundraiser at the California home of Saul Fox. The event was co-hosted by the "Children of Israel," an organization to which Fox was identified as a main donor. The "Children of Israel" organization reportedly donated $734,000 to the Republican party by August 2016, including a $400,000 donation to the Super PAC Stand for Truth, which supported Ted Cruz's presidential campaign, and $334,000 to the Republican National Committee (RNC). Saul Fox was identified as the sole person behind the money channeled through the "Children of Israel" organization, which had used a "ghost corporation" to conceal the identity of its main donor.

Beyond the "Children of Israel" organization, Fox also made personal donations in 2016, including $100,000 to House Speaker Paul Ryan's "Team Ryan" fundraiser and a $25,000 donation to Trump's joint fundraising committee. His 2016 donations also included $2,700 to Mike Huckabee's presidential bid and the maximum $5,400 to the campaigns of Ted Cruz and Marco Rubio. The "Children of Israel" organization also contributed $50,000 to Pursuing America's Greatness, a super PAC supporting Huckabee's campaign, and an additional $100,000 to a pro-Huckabee group in 2015.

It is important to note that while the user requested information on "FOX, SAM," the concrete evidence found for a California political donor with documented ties to pro-Israel advocacy refers to "Saul Fox."

While there is another prominent political donor named Sam Fox, he is primarily associated with St. Louis, Missouri, and served as the national chairman of the Republican Jewish Coalition from 2001 to 2006. This Sam Fox also accompanied House Majority Leader Tom DeLay on a trip to Israel in 2003 and was part of an honorary delegation to Jerusalem for Israel's 60th anniversary in 2008. However, the user's specific request for a *California* political donor is met by Saul Fox.
