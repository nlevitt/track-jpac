# Contributor Report: Stephen Silberstein

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Stephen "Steve" Silberstein is a California-based political donor and philanthropist known for his support of left-of-center causes and organizations. His philanthropic efforts often focus on issues such as income inequality and electoral reform, with significant donations to progressive policy and advocacy groups.

Regarding documented relationships to the Israel lobby and pro-Israel advocacy, Stephen Silberstein has been identified as a supporter of J Street. J Street is an organization that describes itself as the "political home for pro-Israel, pro-peace Americans," advocating for a two-state solution to the Israeli-Palestinian conflict.

The Stephen M. Silberstein Foundation, established in May 1998, has listed J Street among its recipients of donations. Furthermore, Stephen Silberstein is individually listed as a donor or member within J Street's financial statements and donor lists, including their "Tikva Society" which comprises leaders committed to J Street's future.

One source also broadly categorizes Steve Silberstein as a "supporter of liberal Zionist causes." This categorization suggests an alignment with a segment of pro-Israel thought that often supports a two-state solution and may be open to "real pressure on Israel" in the context of peace efforts. However, the most direct and concrete evidence of his involvement in pro-Israel advocacy is through his documented support for J Street.
