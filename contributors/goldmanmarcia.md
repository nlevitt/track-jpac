# Contributor Report: Marcia Goldman

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Marcia Goldman, a California political donor, is associated with the John and Marcia Goldman Foundation, which has provided grants to organizations involved in Jewish community affairs and support for Israeli institutions.

The John and Marcia Goldman Foundation is listed as a supporter of the Jewish Community Federation and Endowment Fund. The Jewish Community Federation of the Bay Area's 2023 Annual Report states that "Upholding Israeli democracy remained a paramount focus, prioritized through on-the-ground partnerships".

Additionally, the John & Marcia Goldman Foundation has provided funding to the "American Friends of the Israel Museum" between 2018 and 2020. The Israel Museum is located in Jerusalem, Israel.

The John and Marcia Goldman Foundation also supports "Jewish community" and "Jewish affairs" in its grantmaking. For instance, the foundation awarded a $200,000 grant to "J., the Jewish news weekly of Northern California," with John Goldman stating the publication served as a "chronicler of the Jewish community". John and Marcia Goldman also established the South Peninsula Jewish Community Teen Foundation to engage Jewish youth in philanthropy.
