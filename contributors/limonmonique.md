Fisher is described as a prominent donor who, through family entities and philanthropic funds, supports pro-Israel advocacy and Israeli institutions via partnerships with the Koret Foundation, contributions to Birthright Israel and AIPAC, and the funding of Israeli political figures.
*   **Robert J. Fisher**: Contributed $2,000 in 2025. Robert J. Fisher is identified as a prominent donor whose family network has documented ties to pro-Israel organizations and Zionist advocacy, including significant financial support to Birthright Israel, involvement with the Koret Foundation, and contributions to pro-Israel political candidates and anti-delegitimization efforts.
*   **John Fisher**: Contributed $1,500 in 2021. John Fisher is noted as a major donor to AIPAC and Birthright Israel Foundation, who also leverages family foundations to support Zionist identity programming and anti-BDS initiatives.
*   **Richard Leib**: Contributed $835 in 2021. Leib is described as a prominent advocate for Zionist causes, who actively used their position as a UC Regent to oppose anti-Zionist activities and maintain ties with pro-Israel organizations.
*   **Anita Friedman**: Contributed $250 in 2021. Friedman is characterized as a primary leader in the pro-Israel lobby, holding high-level positions in major Zionist organizations, directing strategic political contributions, and leveraging philanthropic influence to fortify U.S.-Israel bonds.

These contributions reflect a financial relationship between Monique Limón's political campaigns and individuals identified with the Israel lobby and pro-Israel advocacy efforts.
