*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Harold Matzner was a California-based entrepreneur, humanitarian, and philanthropist known for his extensive charitable contributions across various sectors in the Coachella Valley. His philanthropic efforts extended to a wide range of organizations, including those involved in pro-Israel advocacy.

Documentation indicates that Matzner provided financial support to the American Israel Public Affairs Committee (AIPAC) and StandWithUs. These organizations are recognized for their work in advocating for pro-Israel policies and educating the public about Israel. AIPAC is a prominent lobbying group that advocates for pro-Israel policies in the U.S. Congress, while StandWithUs focuses on Israel education and combating antisemitism.

Matzner's philanthropic activities, which exceeded $70 million to area non-profits since 2000, encompassed a diverse array of causes, including arts and culture, healthcare, education, and social services. His support for pro-Israel organizations was part of a broader pattern of charitable giving.
