# Contributor Report: Farber, Jeff

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Jeff Farber, a figure in California's Jewish community, has demonstrated a documented relationship with the Israel lobby and pro-Israel advocacy through various leadership and philanthropic roles.

Farber has served as the Northern California Chair of the Political Leadership Network (PLN), an organization focused on strengthening congressional support for Israel-related issues through political involvement. Additionally, he has held positions on the National Executive Committee of the American Israel Public Affairs Committee (AIPAC) and has chaired the AIPAC Northern California Board.

His engagement extends to philanthropic endeavors. Farber has served as the executive director of the Koret Foundation, which annually grants over $21 million to Jewish and Israel-related causes. He has also been on the board of directors for the American Friends of Koret Israel Economic Development funds.

In 2004, Jeff and Barbara Farber co-chaired "Israel in the Ballpark," an event highlighting their commitment to Israeli-Bay Area relations.
