# Contributor Report: SCHRAYER, LYNN S

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Lynn S. Schrayer has a documented history of involvement in pro-Israel advocacy through leadership roles in prominent organizations associated with the Israel lobby.

Schrayer served as the National Political Director for the American Israel Public Affairs Committee (AIPAC) for over a decade. AIPAC is recognized as a significant pro-Israel lobbying organization in the United States. During her tenure at AIPAC, a 1988 Washington Post report, based on internal AIPAC documents, indicated that Elizabeth Schrayer (likely Lynn S. Schrayer) attempted to direct Political Action Committee (PAC) donations to specific Senate candidates during the 1986 elections. AIPAC characterized this action as a "rare exception" to its stated policy but maintained it was a legal "communication" due to the interactions being with other AIPAC members within individual PACs.

Currently, Lynn Schrayer holds the position of Secretary and Nominating Vice Chair on the Board of Directors of the Jewish Democratic Council of America (JDCA). The JDCA's platform includes support for a strong U.S.-Israel relationship.
