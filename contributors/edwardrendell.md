# Contributor Report: RENDELL, ED

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Edward G. ("Ed") Rendell, the former Governor of Pennsylvania (2003–2011) and former Mayor of Philadelphia (1992–2000), is a prominent figure in the Democratic Party with documented ties to pro-Israel advocacy and the Israel lobby. While primarily a Pennsylvania-based politician, Rendell has been active as a political contributor and fundraiser in California-linked national politics, most notably as the General Chairman of the Democratic National Committee (DNC) during the 2000 National Convention held in Los Angeles, California.

### 1. Documented Pro-Israel Advocacy and Fundraising
Rendell is a consistent and vocal supporter of the State of Israel, frequently framing support for the country as a moral and political obligation for American Jews and the Democratic Party.

*   **Fundraising for the Israeli Defense Forces (IDF):** In November 2011, Rendell participated in a high-profile fundraiser for the Friends of the Israel Defense Forces (FIDF) in Philadelphia. During the event, he asserted that American Jews have an "obligation" to support Israel "through thick and thin," a stance that drew criticism from advocates for a more balanced U.S. Middle East policy.
    *   [Source: Mondoweiss - "NBC and the Israel lobby"](https://mondoweiss.net/2011/11/nbc-and-the-israel-lobby/)
*   **Support for the Israel Lobby:** Rendell’s political career has been characterized by a close relationship with major pro-Israel organizations. During the 2000 Democratic National Convention in Los Angeles, which Rendell "guided" as DNC Chair, the pro-Israel lobby AIPAC co-hosted a "remarkable display of Jewish unity" event alongside the National Jewish Democratic Council (NJDC) and the United Jewish Communities.
    *   [Source: Jewish Telegraphic Agency (JTA) - "Jewish Democrats prepare to party"](https://www.jta.org/2000/08/08/lifestyle/jewish-democrats-prepare-to-party)
*   **Alignment with Pro-Israel Candidates:** Rendell has used his political influence to endorse and fundraise for candidates prioritized by the Israel lobby. He is noted as an "avid supporter of Israel" who has recently backed establishment favorites in races where pro-Israel advocacy was a central theme, such as the 2026 congressional bid of Sharif Street in Pennsylvania.
    *   [Source: City & State PA - "The Pennsylvania Power 100"](https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2025/06/aws-govcloud-gets-high-level-security-approvals-anthropic-and-meta-ai-models/405995/)

### 2. Strategic Ties to the Israel Lobby Infrastructure
Rendell’s influence within the Israel lobby is also mediated through his long-term associates and professional roles.

*   **Relationship with David L. Cohen:** Rendell’s long-time political advisor and "guru," David L. Cohen (now the U.S. Ambassador to Canada and a former Comcast executive), is a prominent pro-Israel advocate. Cohen formerly served as the vice-chair of the Jewish Federations of Greater Philadelphia, an organization Rendell has frequently supported in its pro-Israel missions.
    *   [Source: Mondoweiss - "David Cohen and Ed Rendell's ties to pro-Israel religion"](https://mondoweiss.net/2011/11/nbc-and-the-israel-lobby/)
*   **Security and Intelligence Contracts:** As Governor, Rendell faced scrutiny for a $125,000 state contract with the Institute of Terrorism Research and Response (ITRR), an Israeli-American agency. The institute, which had offices in Jerusalem and was led by individuals with backgrounds in Israeli security services, was hired to track "potential terrorist threats," which famously included monitoring peaceful domestic protests.
    *   [Source: Forward - "Rendell Dissociates From Firm That Called Peaceful Protests Terrorism"](https://forward.com/schmooze/131276/jewish-ball-player-most-likely-to-cure-cancer-and/)

### 3. Political Contributions and California Connections
Though primarily a recipient of large-scale donations from California-based donors—such as supermarket mogul Ron Burkle—Rendell’s role in California campaign finance is documented through his leadership in the 2000 DNC Host Committee in Los Angeles.

*   **Fundraising and Contributions:** Rendell is listed in campaign finance databases as both a donor and a major fundraiser who facilitated the flow of millions of dollars from California donors into the Democratic Party’s national pro-Israel platform.
