# Contributor Report: Jonathan Lavine

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Jonathan Lavine, a California political donor and co-managing partner at Bain Capital, has a documented history of financial contributions and involvement with organizations engaged in pro-Israel advocacy and initiatives.

Lavine, along with his wife Jeannie Lavine, has provided financial support to the Anti-Defamation League (ADL), an organization identified as a "pro-Israel lobbying and propaganda organization". In recognition of his civic leadership and community service, the ADL honored Jonathan Lavine with its Distinguished Community Service Award in 2012.

The Lavines have consistently supported Combined Jewish Philanthropies (CJP), demonstrating an increased focus in recent years on "investment in Israel, and in the Jewish future". Their contributions to CJP's programs, such as "Onward Israel Boston" and "Alternative Spring Break," are aimed at fostering connections between Jewish young adults and Israel. These initiatives are described as helping "to build the connection that Jewish young adults have with Israel". One source critically states that their donations to CJP's programs "support and normalize Israel's ethnic cleansing of Palestinians from their homeland and theft of Palestinian resources".

Beyond the ADL and CJP, Jonathan and Jeannie Lavine support other Jewish organizations, including Brandeis University, the American World Jewish Service, and the Kraft Center for Jewish Student Life.

In 2016, an email released by WikiLeaks indicated that Jonathan Lavine joined the board of the Center for American Progress (CAP) following an invitation extended to then-Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. The communication suggested that securing Lavine, described as a donor who "loves Israel," was a beneficial outcome of the event.

Lavine has also made a six-figure contribution to the Lincoln Project, an American political action committee. Additionally, he provided a $100,000 donation to the Jewish Democratic Council of America (JDCA). The JDCA's PAC is capable of both donating to candidates and running advertisements.
