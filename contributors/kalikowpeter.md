# Contributor Report: KALIKOW, PETER S

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Peter S. Kalikow has a documented history of engagement with organizations and initiatives that support Israel and pro-Israel advocacy. His contributions and affiliations indicate a sustained involvement in efforts aimed at Israel's development and the promotion of Jewish interests.

Key documented relationships and activities include:

*   **Israel Bonds** Kalikow was awarded the "Peace Medal," described as Israel's highest civilian award, in 1982 for his efforts in aiding the nation's development, specifically citing his involvement with Israel Bonds. He also served as the General Chairman of the Real Estate Division of Israel Bonds for eight years.
*   **Jewish National Fund** He has served on the board of directors of the Jewish National Fund (JNF). The JNF is an organization focused on land development and forestry in Israel.
*   **Museum of Jewish Heritage** Kalikow is a founding trustee of the Museum of Jewish Heritage in New York City.
*   **UJA-Federation and Anti-Defamation League** He has been recognized with awards from the UJA-Federation and the Anti-Defamation League of the B'nai B'rith. These organizations are significant in American Jewish philanthropy and advocacy, including support for Israel and combating antisemitism.
*   **Temple Emanu-El** Kalikow has made donations to Temple Emanu-El, a prominent Reform synagogue in New York City, leading to the establishment of the "Peter and Mary Kalikow Senior Rabbinic Chair". The current holder of this position, Rabbi Joshua M. Davidson, has publicly addressed topics such as "Progressivism's Breakdown Over Israel and Jews".
*   **Family Philanthropy** The Kalikow family's philanthropic history includes the founding of the Kalikow Family Circle in 1921, an organization that donated to local Jewish organizations.
