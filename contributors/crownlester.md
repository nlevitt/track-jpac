# Contributor Report: CROWN, LESTER

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Lester Crown is a Chicago-based billionaire and chairman emeritus of Henry Crown and Company. While his primary residence and business operations are centered in Illinois, he is a prominent national political donor with documented financial and advocacy influence in California politics, particularly regarding pro-Israel interests. He is widely recognized as a "patriarch" of the Jewish community in Chicago and a pivotal figure in U.S.-Israel relations.

### 1. Relationship to the Israel Lobby and Advocacy Groups
Crown maintains deep institutional and financial ties to major pro-Israel advocacy and research organizations. His involvement spans leadership roles, major philanthropic endowments, and direct political lobbying.

*   **Institutional Leadership:** Crown serves as the Deputy Chair of the International Board of the **Weizmann Institute of Science** in Israel and is a member of the Board of Governors for **Tel Aviv University** [1.7, 1.10].
*   **Philanthropic Endowments:** In 2005, Crown established the **Crown Center for Middle East Studies** at Brandeis University with a $10 million gift [1.5, 1.7]. Critics have alleged that his position on the board of **General Dynamics**—a major defense contractor with extensive ties to the Israeli military—could influence the center's research agenda [1.8]. 
*   **Direct Advocacy and Lobbying:** Crown has a documented history of participating in high-level pro-Israel advocacy. He has signed public letters to Israeli leadership, such as a 2013 letter to Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu urging "pragmatic initiatives" for a two-state solution while emphasizing Israel's security needs [1.7]. He is also noted for his regular attendance and speeches at **AIPAC** (American Israel Public Affairs Committee) events [1.4].

### 2. Political Contributions and "Bundling"
Crown is a prolific "bundler" and donor for the Democratic Party, often leveraging his contributions to ensure candidates maintain a staunchly pro-Israel platform.

*   **Vouching for Presidential Candidates:** During the 2008 presidential campaign, Crown played a critical role in "reassuring" Jewish donors about Barack Obama’s commitment to Israel. He sent mass emails to voters and donors, describing Obama's record on Israel as "stellar" and his dedication to Israel's security as "unyielding" [1.5, 1.11].
*   **California-Specific Contributions:** Crown has frequently funded California politicians who hold influential positions on foreign policy.
    *   **Brad Sherman (D-CA):** Documented personal donations include $2,800 in 2022 and ongoing support through his firm, Henry Crown and Company [1.4, 1.12].
    *   **Howard Berman (D-CA):** Crown was a major backer of former Rep. Howard Berman, the long-time chair of the House Foreign Affairs Committee, specifically in high-stakes "pro-Israel donor" battles against other Democrats [1.12].
*   **Funding Pro-Israel Entities:** Through the Crown Family Philanthropies, he provides significant funding to the **American Friends of the IDF** (Israel Defense Forces) and the **Jerusalem Foundation** [1.6, 1.11].

### 3. Documented Influence on U.S. Foreign Policy
Crown’s relationship with the "Israel lobby" extends to serving as a conduit for Israeli security concerns to the U.S. Executive Branch.

*   **Intelligence Liaison:** In 2010, it was reported that Israeli military intelligence chief **Major General Amos Yadlin** visited Crown in Chicago to ask him to convey Israel's specific concerns regarding Iran's nuclear program directly to President Obama [1.11].
*   **Policy Advocacy:** Crown has utilized his leadership roles in the **Chicago Council on Global Affairs** and the **Council on Foreign Relations** to promote hawkish positions on Iran, aligning with Israeli security priorities [1.13].
