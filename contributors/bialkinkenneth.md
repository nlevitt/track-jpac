# Contributor Report: BIALKIN, KENNETH J

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Kenneth J. Bialkin (1929-2019) was an American attorney and a prominent figure in various Jewish organizations, with a documented history of leadership and advocacy focused on supporting Israel and enhancing U.S.-Israel relations.

Bialkin held numerous leadership positions within organizations that actively advocate for Israel and Jewish causes. He served as Chairman of the Conference of Presidents of Major American Jewish Organizations from 1984 to 1986, an organization that addresses issues including "Israel's security and vitality". He was also National Chairman of the Anti-Defamation League (ADL) from 1982 to 1986, an organization described as fighting antisemitism and supporting "the safety and security of the Jewish people and the state of Israel".

His involvement extended to direct roles in U.S.-Israel relations, including serving as Chairman of the America-Israel Friendship League and Vice Chairman of the Jerusalem Foundation for nearly three decades. Bialkin was also a board member of the Republican Jewish Coalition and President of the Jewish Community Relations Council of New York.

Bialkin's personal statements and actions further illustrate his commitment to pro-Israel advocacy. His wife, Ann Bialkin, noted that he "always does things... for a cause he believes in, especially when it's about Israel or the Jewish people". He was described as having "fought and presented Israel's case to American presidents, world leaders, to the media, to American Jewry and to Muslims". Bialkin stated his strong identification with Israel's "need to make a Jewish home for the Jewish people and their fight was my fight". He also indicated his belief that "Israel has no stronger friend than the US, and the US has no stronger friend than Israel". In a discussion with Prime Minister Ehud Barak, Bialkin conveyed that proposed concessions "broke my heart," but he would support them if they guaranteed the end of the Palestinian conflict. He also subscribed to the view that "Israel-bashing is a new outlet of Jew-hatred".

In 2017, Bialkin reportedly threatened to withdraw his financial support from The New School over a panel discussion he characterized as potentially propagating "animus and bigotry towards the Jewish people" and "institutionalized the anti-Semitism of the radical left".

Philanthropically, Kenneth and Ann Bialkin founded the Bialkin Family Foundation, which explicitly states its support for "Zionism (the political ideology that the Jewish people should have a national home in the Holy Land) and the enhancement of relations between the United States and Israel". Major recipients of the foundation's grants include ELEM: Youth in Distress, which aids at-risk Israeli youth, and the American Israel Friendship League. Ann Bialkin also established ELEM, an organization assisting teenagers in Israel.
