# Contributor Report: DEBBIE WASSERMAN SHULTZ FOR CONGRESS

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

"DEBBIE WASSERMAN SHULTZ FOR CONGRESS" (officially **Debbie Wasserman Schultz for Congress**, FEC ID: C00385773) is the principal campaign committee for U.S. Representative Debbie Wasserman Schultz (D-FL). While she represents Florida's 25th district, the committee is documented as a political donor in California state records due to its contributions to California-based candidates and party organizations. The committee maintains an extensive and documented relationship with the Israel lobby and is consistently ranked among the most active pro-Israel entities in the U.S. House of Representatives.

### 1. Documentation of California Political Activity
The committee is registered as an out-of-state donor in California's campaign finance systems. Documentation shows the committee has diverted funds to California political entities to support pro-Israel or Democratic Party interests:
*   **California Democratic Party:** The committee has made multiple contributions to the California Democratic Party, including an $8,500 donation in late 2013 and $6,903 in 2023.

    *   *Source: The Guardian - Sports Owners/Political Donations Data (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)*
*   **Candidate Contributions:** The committee has historically provided financial support to pro-Israel California incumbents, including Representative Eric Swalwell and Representative Adam Schiff.
    *   *Source: [FEC Committee Overview - DEBBIE WASSERMAN SCHULTZ FOR CONGRESS](https://www.fec.gov/data/committee/C00385773/)*

### 2. Relationship to the Israel Lobby
The committee and its principal are deeply integrated into the "Israel lobby" infrastructure, specifically through the American Israel Public Affairs Committee (AIPAC) and Democratic Majority for Israel (DMFI).

#### Financial Support from Pro-Israel Groups
*   **AIPAC and Pro-Israel America:** Wasserman Schultz is a consistent recipient of funding from donors organized by AIPAC. In the 2024 cycle, she was listed as a "top candidate" by Pro-Israel America, a PAC closely aligned with AIPAC's mission.
    *   *Source: OpenSecrets - Debbie Wasserman Schultz: Donor Sector Profile (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)*
*   **Democratic Majority for Israel (DMFI):** Her committee receives significant support from DMFI, an organization dedicated to maintaining pro-Israel support within the Democratic Party.
    *   *Source: DMFI PAC Endorsements - 2024 Cycle (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)*

#### Institutional and Legislative Advocacy
The committee's relationship to the lobby is evidenced by her leadership in pro-Israel legislative caucuses and initiatives:
*   **Abraham Accords Caucus:** Wasserman Schultz serves as the Democratic co-chair of the Abraham Accords Caucus, which advocates for the expansion of normalization agreements between Israel and Arab nations.
    *   *Source: [Official House Website - Abraham Accords Caucus](https://wassermanschultz.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=2415)*
*   **Bipartisan Task Force for Combating Antisemitism:** She co-founded this task force, which often aligns its definitions and legislative priorities with those of the Anti-Defamation League (ADL) and AIPAC.
    *   *Source: Wasserman Schultz Press Release - Jewish American Heritage (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)*
*   **Censure of Critics:** In November 2023, she was one of the 22 Democrats who voted to censure Representative Rashida Tlaib for her rhetoric regarding the Israel-Hamas war, a move heavily advocated for by pro-Israel lobbying groups.
    *   *Source: Wikipedia - Debbie Wasserman Schultz: Israeli–Palestinian conflict (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)*

### 3. Critical Nuance: Lobbying Pressure and Sanctions
The relationship between the committee and the Israel lobby has occasionally been characterized by high-pressure tactics. In 2014, AIPAC launched a documented pressure campaign against Wasserman Schultz when she initially resisted supporting new Iran sanctions that were opposed by the Obama administration.
*   **AIPAC "Squeeze":** AIPAC activists in her district were reportedly urged to "respectfully ask" her why she was blocking the sanctions. After negotiations, AIPAC issued a corrective statement affirming she was a "good friend of Israel and a close friend of AIPAC," demonstrating the lobby's influence over her public positioning.
    *   *Source: The Forward - Debbie Wasserman Schultz Squeezed Hard as Iran Sanctions Fight Heats Up (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)*
    *   *Source: [Times of Israel - AIPAC Rejects Report of Differences with Wasserman Schultz](https://www.timesofisrael.com/aipac-rejects-report-of-differences-with-wasserman-schultz/)*

### 4. Recent Legislative Actions
Following the October 7, 2023 attacks, the committee's legislative record has shifted toward unconditional support for Israeli military operations:
*   **Military Aid:** She voted in favor of an additional $14.3 billion in military aid to Israel in late 2023.
    *   *Source: LegiScan - House Resolution 6126 Voting Record (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)*
*   **Anti-BDS and Antisemitism Legislation:** She has sponsored or co-sponsored various bills targeting the Boycott, Divestment, and Sanctions (BDS) movement and expanding the definition of antisemitism to include certain criticisms of Israel.
    *   *Source: Congress.gov - Legislation Sponsored by Rep. Wasserman Schultz (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)*
