# Contributor Report: Jonathan M. Tisch

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Jonathan M. Tisch, an American businessman and co-chairman of the Board of Loews Corporation, has a documented relationship to pro-Israel advocacy primarily through political donations aimed at opposing candidates critical of Israel.

In 2025, Jonathan M. Tisch and his wife, Elizabeth Tisch, donated $250,000 to "Fix the City," a super PAC. This super PAC focused on opposing the mayoral candidacy of Zohran Mamdani in New York City. Mamdani, a democratic socialist who ultimately won the election, has been identified as a proponent of Palestinian rights, a supporter of the Boycott, Divestment and Sanctions (BDS) movement, and an opponent of U.S. military aid to Israel. He has also stated his rejection of Israel's identity as a Jewish state and pledged to arrest Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu if he were to land in New York.

The broader context of wealthy donors opposing progressive candidates like Mamdani has been linked to such candidates' "advocacy for Palestinians". "Fix the City" was initially established to support former Governor Andrew Cuomo's mayoral bid before expanding its mission to oppose Mamdani.

Further demonstrating financial support for pro-Israel political efforts within the Tisch family, Jonathan M. Tisch's wife, Lizzie Tisch, donated $24,000 to "Solidarity PAC". This organization is described as a "pro-Israel fundraising group" that directs donors to candidates who align with its "deep-rooted American values" and "continued support for" Israel. Solidarity PAC also maintains a "Wall of Shame" for officials critical of Israel's actions in Gaza.
