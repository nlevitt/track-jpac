# Contributor Report: John Goldman

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

John Goldman is a California-based philanthropist known for his involvement in various civic and Jewish communal organizations. He previously served as president of the San Francisco-based Jewish Community Federation and currently sits on its Board of Governors.

Through the John and Marcia Goldman Foundation, he has supported a range of organizations, including the New Israel Fund. The New Israel Fund is an organization that advocates for a democratic Israel and supports civil and human rights within the country. His philanthropic efforts, particularly through the foundation, demonstrate support for organizations engaged in pro-Israel advocacy within a progressive framework.
