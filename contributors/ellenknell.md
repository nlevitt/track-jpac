# Contributor Report: Ellen R. Knell

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Ellen R. Knell is a political donor and geneticist specializing in cancer-risk assessment, recognized for her philanthropic activities. Her documented relationship to pro-Israel advocacy is primarily evidenced through her substantial support and engagement with the Weizmann Institute of Science, a prominent research institution located in Rehovot, Israel.

Dr. Knell, alongside her husband Harvey Knell, has been acknowledged for their significant contributions to the Weizmann Institute. In 2022, they were awarded the Doctor of Philosophy *honoris causa*, the Institute's highest recognition, for their leadership, friendship, and extraordinary contributions to scientific discovery and humanity. The American Committee for the Weizmann Institute of Science (ACWIS) honored Dr. Ellen Knell for her "devotion to the Jewish people and Israel".

Their philanthropic endeavors include the establishment of the Knell Family Professorial Chair and the Knell Family Center for Microbiology at the Weizmann Institute. These initiatives support basic research and multidisciplinary scientific investigations within Israel. Dr. Knell and her husband are described as "pillars of the Weizmann family for 30 years and members of the President's Circle," demonstrating sustained support and advocacy for the institution. They are also long-time members of the Weizmann family, through their dedicated service on the Institute's International Board, and have made numerous contributions in areas including "Jewish causes" through their philanthropic foundation. Harvey Knell, her husband, has served as a National Board member for the American Committee and currently holds the position of Chair of the National Board, further solidifying their interconnectedness with this Israeli institution.
