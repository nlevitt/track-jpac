# Contributor Report: Stephen A. Cozen

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Stephen A. Cozen (1939–2024) was a prominent attorney, political fundraiser, and the founder and chairman of the Philadelphia-based law firm **Cozen O’Connor**. While primarily based in Pennsylvania, Cozen held significant leadership roles in California-based institutions and was a prolific donor to political and philanthropic organizations, with a documented focus on pro-Israel advocacy and Jewish communal interests.

### 1. Leadership in Pro-Israel and Jewish Advocacy Organizations
Cozen held high-level leadership positions in several organizations central to the Israel lobby and Jewish advocacy in the United States.

*   **USC Shoah Foundation (Los Angeles, CA):** Cozen served as the **Chairman of the Board of Councilors** (2015–2019) for the USC Shoah Foundation, an institute based in California dedicated to documenting the Holocaust and other genocides. Under his leadership, the foundation launched a **$100 million capital campaign** and expanded its mission to combat global antisemitism.
    *   Source: USC Shoah Foundation (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)
    *   [Source: University of Pennsylvania](https://www.law.upenn.edu/live/files/13656-ctic-news-views-november-2023-pdf)
*   **Jewish Federation of Greater Philadelphia:** Cozen was a "major supporter" and long-term leader within the Federation. In 2008, he explicitly defined the organization's success as being rooted in three strategic pillars, the first of which was the **"support of Israel."**
    *   Source: Cozen.com (Archived Jewish Federation Feature) (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)
*   **Anti-Defamation League (ADL):** In 2002, Cozen received the **ADL’s highest honor**, the "Americanism Award," in recognition of his leadership and philanthropic contributions to the organization's mission, which includes pro-Israel advocacy and monitoring anti-Israel sentiment.
    *   Source: Cozen O'Connor Bio (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)
*   **Weitzman National Museum of American Jewish History:** Cozen served as a board member and Vice Chair of this institution. In 2024, he was the inaugural recipient of the "Only in America Humanitarian Award," cited for his financial and "political support" for the museum.
    *   [Source: Philadelphia Jewish Exponent](https://www.jewishexponent.com/steve-cozen-to-be-honored-by-weitzman-museum/)

### 2. Documented Advocacy and Personal Ties to Israel
Cozen maintained direct ties to the Israeli defense establishment and integrated pro-Israel advocacy into his professional and personal life.

*   **Relationship with Israeli Military Leadership:** Cozen maintained a close multi-decade relationship with **Yoram "YaYa" Yair**, a retired Major General in the **Israel Defense Forces (IDF)**. The two reportedly collaborated on various "business and otherwise" projects.
    *   Source: Cozen.com (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)
*   **Cozen O’Connor Israel Practice:** Under Cozen's chairmanship, his law firm established a dedicated **Israel Practice Group** to facilitate cross-border transactions and support Israeli companies and investors in the U.S. market.
    *   Source: Grokipedia / Cozen O'Connor (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)
*   **Visual and Symbolic Advocacy:** Cozen’s office featured artifacts reflecting his commitment to Israel, including two tablets from the **Western Wall (Kotel)** and a signed photograph of the **Oslo Peace Accords** given to him by Bill Clinton. He frequently participated in "President's Missions" to Israel organized by the Jewish Federation.
    *   Source: Cozen.com (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)

### 3. Political Fundraising and Influence
Cozen was a "top Democratic fundraiser" and adviser, notably for **President Joe Biden**. While he donated heavily to individual candidates and the Democratic Party, his political influence was often channeled through the network of pro-Israel donors in Philadelphia and California.

*   **Political Spending:** Between 2011 and 2021, Cozen individually donated over **$432,000** to various political committees and candidates, making him one of the most prolific donors among high-profile U.S. lawyers.
    *   [Source: WHYY / Spotlight PA](https://whyy.org/articles/pa-lawmakers-hand-out-millions-in-public-contracts-to-law-firms-that-fill-their-campaign-coffers/)
*   **PAC Contributions:** His firm's PAC, the **Cozen O’Connor PAC**, is active in federal and state elections, directing funds toward candidates who align with the firm's strategic and bipartisan interests, including pro-Israel legislative goals.
    *   Source: Grokipedia (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)
*   **Philanthropic Donations to Reconciliation Projects:** Cozen was a documented donor to **Seeds of Peace**, an organization that brings together Israeli and Palestinian youth, which is often supported by centrist pro-Israel donors.
    *   Source: Seeds of Peace 2004 Report (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)
