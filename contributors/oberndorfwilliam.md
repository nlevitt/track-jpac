# Contributor Report: Oberndorf, William

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

William Ernst Oberndorf (commonly known as Bill Oberndorf) is a San Francisco-based billionaire hedge fund manager and a significant donor to Republican and centrist Democratic causes. While much of his public profile focuses on education reform and California-specific political recalls, he is documented as a member of a network of donors that supports pro-Israel advocacy and candidates aligned with the Israel lobby.

### 1. Direct Philanthropic Support for Pro-Israel Organizations
Through the **Oberndorf Family Foundation**, Oberndorf has provided financial support to organizations specifically focused on Israel and its relationship with the United States. 

*   **American-Israel Democracy Coalition (Brothers & Sisters for Israel):** In 2024, the Oberndorf Family Foundation issued a grant of **$5,000** to the American-Israel Democracy Coalition, an organization affiliated with the Israeli group *Achiot VeAchim L’Israel* (Brothers and Sisters in Arms), which pivoted from domestic protests to high-profile advocacy and humanitarian efforts following the October 7 attacks.

### 2. Association with the Pro-Israel Donor Network
Political reporting and industry trackers have identified Oberndorf as part of a specific cohort of "pro-Israel megadonors" whose contributions are frequently funneled toward candidates who support unconditional U.S. military aid to Israel.

*   **Funding Pro-Israel Candidates:** Oberndorf is cited alongside prominent pro-Israel donors such as Haim Saban and Paul Singer as a key financial backer of Senator **Cory Booker**. Booker is noted for his vocal support of Israeli military policy and his receipt of millions in support from groups like the **American Israel Public Affairs Committee (AIPAC)** and **Democratic Majority for Israel (DMFI)**.
    *   *Source: [NJToday - Booker Bats for Billionaires](https://njtoday.news/2025/12/17/booker-bats-for-billionaires-but-betrays-bottom-80-percent-of-americans/)*
*   **Presence in Lobbying Circles:** Oberndorf and his wife, Susan Oberndorf, are frequently documented in reports tracking the activities of the pro-Israel lobby. They have been listed as attendees or affiliates of major pro-Israel events, including those hosted by AIPAC.
    *   *Source: [Jewish Insider - Daily Kickoff (June 26, 2019)](https://www.timesofisrael.com/jewish-insiders-daily-kickoff-june-26-2019/)*
    *   *Source: Jewish Insider - Daily Kickoff (June 28, 2017) (Note: having trouble producing a specific citation)*

### 3. Contextual Political Alignment
Oberndorf’s political expenditures often align with organizations that prioritize a strong U.S.-Israel relationship as a core tenet of their foreign policy platform.

*   **Conservative Leadership Fund Support:** Oberndorf has donated millions to the **Republican Senate Leadership Fund** and other PACs that consistently advocate for a "pro-Israel" hawkish foreign policy, including opposition to the Iran Nuclear Deal—a primary objective of the Israel lobby.
