# Contributor Report: Ronald H. Kaufman

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Ronald H. Kaufman of San Francisco, California, is a prominent figure in commercial real estate and a notable donor with documented ties to pro-Israel advocacy. His involvement includes leadership roles in Jewish community organizations and direct participation in pro-Israel lobbying efforts.

Kaufman, who owns The Ron Kaufman Companies, a commercial real estate firm, has served as co-chair of the regional chapter of the American Israel Public Affairs Committee (AIPAC) with his wife, Barbara. His engagement with Israel also includes leading a couples mission to the country in April 1974, shortly after the Yom Kippur War. During this trip, Kaufman had his adult Bar Mitzvah on Masada.

Beyond AIPAC, Kaufman has held leadership positions in various Jewish organizations, including serving as president of the Jewish Community Federation and Jewish Family and Children's Services of San Francisco. He has also been involved with the Jewish Vocational Service and the Hebrew Immigrant Aid Society. His philanthropic and civic activities extend to other areas, such as the California Pacific Medical Center, the Bernard Osher Foundation, United Way, and the UCSF/Mt. Zion Board.
