# Contributor Report: BRONFMAN, EDGAR M

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Research indicates that "BRONFMAN, EDGAR M" most likely refers to Edgar M. Bronfman Sr. (1929-2013), a prominent Canadian-American businessman and philanthropist. While he was a significant political donor at the federal level, specific evidence identifying him primarily as a "California political donor" for state or local campaigns was not found in the available search results. Federal records show Edgar M. Bronfman Sr. personally contributed $200,000 to the Republican National Committee in 1991. His son, Edgar M. Bronfman Jr., also made political contributions, notably "at least $318,000 to candidates or the Democratic National Committee" since 1995, with ties to the entertainment industry.

Edgar M. Bronfman Sr.'s documented relationship to the Israel lobby and pro-Israel advocacy is substantial and spans several decades through his leadership and philanthropic activities:

*   **World Jewish Congress (WJC)**: Edgar M. Bronfman Sr. served as president of the World Jewish Congress for 28 years, from 1979 to 2007, transforming it into a influential organization. In this role, he advocated for Jewish rights globally. The WJC under his leadership was involved in efforts to free Soviet Jews, enabling many to immigrate to Israel.
*   **Donations to Pro-Israel Groups**: Bronfman was a donor to several groups identified as Israel lobby groups, including the Foundation for Defense of Democracies and UN Watch. He reportedly made a gift in excess of $120,000 to AIPAC (American Israel Public Affairs Committee) in 1997, with aides indicating it was intended to influence the lobby towards a more liberal direction and support for the Israeli-Palestinian peace process.
*   **Pro-Israel Advocacy and Initiatives**:
    *   He founded the Bronfman Youth Fellowships in Israel, a leadership program for Jewish youth from America and Israel, established in 1987.
    *   Bronfman publicly advocated for Israel's interests, including campaigning to undo the 1975 UN resolution equating Zionism with racism. He also focused on combating anti-Semitism and promoting Jewish identity, which he linked to the well-being of the Jewish people globally.
    *   In a 2008 article for The Huffington Post, Bronfman wrote about "Israel's Best Interest is a Morally Strong America," critically assessing U.S. policy in the Middle East and arguing for an "honest broker" role to achieve Israeli-Palestinian peace.
*   **Broader Jewish Philanthropy with Israeli Connections**: Bronfman's philanthropic efforts extended to Jewish educational and social programs in the United States and Israel. He helped establish the Edgar M. Bronfman Center for Jewish Student Life at New York University. He was also a benefactor of other Jewish causes, including Hillel: The Foundation for Jewish Campus Life, which he was credited with helping revive. He provided funding for MyJewishLearning.com, an online resource for Jewish education.

In summary, Edgar M. Bronfman Sr. was a prominent figure with extensive and documented involvement in pro-Israel advocacy and support for Jewish organizations, including significant donations to groups associated with the Israel lobby and initiatives directly related to Israel. While he was a documented political donor at the federal level, specific evidence for him being primarily a "California political donor" for state or local campaigns was not found in the provided research.
