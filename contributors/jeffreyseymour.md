# Contributor Report: Jeffrey Seymour

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Jeffrey Seymour, a governmental relations and land use consultant based in Westlake Village, California, has a documented relationship with pro-Israel advocacy through contributions to J Street, an organization that describes itself as pro-Israel and pro-peace.

Jeffrey A. Seymour is identified as the principal of Seymour Consulting Group, a firm specializing in governmental and community relations, planning, zoning, and land development in Southern California. He has a background in both the public and private sectors, including roles with the Los Angeles City Council and the Los Angeles Board of Supervisors. Seymour is also involved with academic and civic organizations, serving on the Board of Advisors of the UCLA Luskin School of Public Affairs and co-founding the California Coalition for Public Higher Education.

Documentation indicates that Valerie and Jeffrey Seymour have contributed to J Street. Their names appear in the donor lists of J Street's financial statements for multiple years, including 2018, 2022, 2023, and 2024. J Street advocates for a two-state solution and describes its mission as promoting "pro-Israel, pro-peace" American leadership to achieve a secure Israel alongside a sovereign Palestinian state.
