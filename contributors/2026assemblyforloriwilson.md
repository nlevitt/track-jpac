# Contributor Report: Lori Wilson for Assembly 2026

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

California Assemblymember Lori Wilson, representing the 11th Assembly District, has received campaign contributions linked to pro-Israel advocacy and has supported legislative measures related to Israeli Americans. Wilson was elected in April 2022 and is seeking re-election in the June 2, 2026 primary election. Her district includes all of Solano County and portions of Contra Costa and Sacramento Counties.

Financial disclosures indicate that Lori Wilson's campaign has received direct contributions from individuals identified as significant donors to pro-Israel causes. As of May 2, 2026, the total documented contributions from sources identified with the Israel lobby amounted to $2,000.

Specific contributions include:
*   William S. Fisher contributed $1,000 in 2021. Fisher is noted as a major donor to Birthright Israel and the Jerusalem Foundation, has associations with the Koret Foundation (a funder of pro-Israel causes), and is a donor to pro-Israel political candidates.
*   Rich Leib contributed $500 in 2023. Leib is described as a prominent advocate for Zionist causes who has actively used his position as a UC Regent to oppose anti-Zionist activities and maintain ties with pro-Israel organizations.
*   Julie Platt contributed $500 in 2023. Platt is a leader of The Jewish Federations of North America, an organization with a documented record of active support for Israel and Zionist advocacy.

In terms of legislative activity, Assemblymember Wilson has supported resolutions concerning Israeli Americans. A record from LegiScan shows that a resolution titled "Relative to Israeli Americans" was introduced, read, and adopted during a session in which Lori Wilson was an Assemblymember.
