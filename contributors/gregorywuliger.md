No documented evidence was found to link Gregory T. Wuliger specifically as a California political donor with a documented relationship to the Israel lobby and pro-Israel advocacy within the state of California.

Research indicates that Gregory T. Wuliger has held significant roles in pro-Israel advocacy organizations and Jewish community leadership, primarily in Cleveland, Ohio. He has served as a member of the American Israel Public Affairs Committee's (AIPAC) National Council, a member of their New Leadership Network, and as AIPAC Cleveland Council's Political Chair. Wuliger has also been involved with the Jewish Federations of North America and the Jewish Federation of Cleveland, including co-chairing the annual Yom Ha'atzmaut Celebration and a Mission to Washington, D.C.. The Wuliger family has also been noted for contributing to projects in Israel through the Jewish Federation of Cleveland, such as a senior adult daycare center in Neve Sharett.

While these activities demonstrate a clear relationship to pro-Israel advocacy and the Israel lobby, the available information does not establish Gregory T. Wuliger as a political donor in California or detail his pro-Israel advocacy in the context of California politics or donations.
