# Contributor Report: Trevor Chandler

*Let us know if you spot an inaccuracy: trackjpac@gmail.com*

Trevor Chandler is a political figure in California who has held various roles, including a recent candidacy for the San Francisco Board of Supervisors and a position on the California State Board of Pharmacy. His professional background includes significant involvement with the American Israel Public Affairs Committee (AIPAC), a prominent pro-Israel advocacy organization.

Chandler served as a Regional Outreach Director for AIPAC from 2017 to 2022. During his tenure, he also held the title of Progressive Outreach Director, a role in which he stated he "facilitated dialogues between progressives, the Palestinian Authority, and Arab Israelis, supporting a two-state solution". He clarified that he was not a lobbyist and did not campaign for any candidates in this role.

AIPAC is widely recognized as a single-issue organization dedicated to electing members of Congress who support the State of Israel, making financial contributions to both Republican and Democratic candidates who align with its mission.

During his campaign for the San Francisco Board of Supervisors, some reports noted that Chandler's work with AIPAC was not consistently highlighted on his campaign website or resume, though it was present on his LinkedIn profile. Chandler publicly addressed his past employment with AIPAC, stating, "I care more about what's happening in the streets of the Mission than in the streets of Gaza".

Regarding foreign policy matters, Chandler expressed opposition to a resolution calling for a Gaza ceasefire by the San Francisco Board of Supervisors. He argued that "Foreign policy doesn't belong to the Board of Supervisors" and suggested that the debate over the resolution contributed to "anti-Semitic sentiments". Among his endorsements for his supervisorial campaign was the Raoul Wallenberg Jewish Democratic Club.
